## Concepts adapted

The provenance workflow adapts these ideas:

- explicit user opt-in and bounded provenance scope;
- two-pass source-landscape and deep-provenance research;
- primary-source preference and source freshness;
- exact identifiers, distinctive phrases, dependencies, disclosed prompts,
  provenance records, and chronology as search leads;
- independent source cross-checks and alternative explanations;
- confidence plus concise evidence bases;
- an inaccessible-resource register and ranked retrieval priorities;
- neutral language and a prohibition on style-based attribution;
- human review before external sharing.

## Human-readable prior-art projection

The local workflow adds a repository-specific, versioned
`prior-art-summary.md` contract. Its mutable latest projection and canonical
public-link table are derived only from admitted evidence and chronology or
search records, while numbered `PRIOR-ART-ITERATION-NNN` blocks preserve
append-only changes in human-facing lineage framing. This presentation
contract is local workflow design; it does not add or reclassify research
evidence by itself.
